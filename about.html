About TIMS Institute

TIMS Institute

TIMS Institute is an educational institution committed to empowering students with practical knowledge and skills that go beyond traditional classroom learning. The institute recognizes that the world is rapidly transforming through technology, and prepares students to be active participants — not passive consumers — of this transformation.

The Coding Kids program is TIMS Institute's flagship skill-development initiative designed specifically for school students, offering structured, practical, and age-appropriate technology education.

"बच्चों को सिर्फ Technology इस्तेमाल करना नहीं,
Technology बनाना सिखाना।"

This is the core idea behind Coding Kids. Every child who uses a smartphone, tablet or computer has the potential to also build technology — if given the right opportunity and the right guidance.

Our Mission

To provide every school student with access to practical, structured, and engaging technology education — covering computers, AI, coding, and web development — so they are prepared for the opportunities of tomorrow.

Our Vision

A generation of young Indians who don't just use technology — but create it. We envision students who graduate with real skills, real projects, and real confidence to build solutions that matter in the digital world.

Philosophy

Our Learning Philosophy

Built on proven educational principles that actually work for students of all ages.

Learn by Doing
Every class includes hands-on practice. Students don't just watch demonstrations — they build things themselves from day one.
Project-Based Learning
Projects are the vehicle for learning. Each concept is taught in the context of building something meaningful — not just memorizing syntax.
Age-Appropriate Curriculum
Each level is designed specifically for the cognitive and academic development stage of the target age group — not one-size-fits-all.
Why AI Literacy Matters
AI tools are already transforming every industry. Students who understand and can use AI tools will have a significant advantage in their careers and academics.
Why Coding for School Students?
Coding teaches logical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving — skills that benefit students in every subject and every career, not just in tech.
Portfolio Over Theory
Every student completes the course with real projects they can show to others — a portfolio that demonstrates actual skill, not just a certificate.
