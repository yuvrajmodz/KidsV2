Process

How the Challenge Works

A simple, fair 4-step process to participate in the Scholarship Challenge.

01
Register
Fill out the scholarship application form online. No fee required to register.
02
Take the Aptitude Challenge
Appear for the aptitude challenge at the designated test center or online, as notified.
03
Get Your Result
Results are shared with registered candidates. Scores determine scholarship eligibility.
04
Scholarship Eligibility
Eligible candidates can apply the scholarship amount against their selected course fee.
Evaluation

Students Will Be Evaluated On

The aptitude challenge tests core thinking skills — no prior coding knowledge is needed.

Logical Thinking
Pattern recognition, sequences, and logical reasoning questions.
Critical Thinking
Analyzing situations, evaluating information, and drawing conclusions.
Problem Solving
Step-by-step thinking to solve practical and mathematical problems.
Computer Awareness
Basic knowledge of computers, devices, and the internet.
AI Awareness
Basic awareness of artificial intelligence, its uses, and impact.
Computational Thinking
Decomposition, patterns, abstraction, and algorithmic thinking basics.
Eligibility

Who Can Participate?

School Students
Any school student from Class 5 to Class 12 who wants to learn technology and coding.
No Coding Background Needed
Prior coding experience is not required. The aptitude test evaluates thinking skills, not technical knowledge.
All Schools Welcome
Students from any school — government, private, or international — can participate in the challenge.
Instructions

Important Instructions

  • Registration is open to all eligible school students. Register only once.
  • The aptitude challenge is a written or digital test. Date and venue will be communicated after registration.
  • Scholarship is applicable only towards the Coding Kids course fee at TIMS Institute.
  • Scholarship eligibility is determined by test scores. There is no guarantee of scholarship.
  • All communication regarding the challenge will be sent to the registered email and phone number.
  • TIMS Institute reserves the right to modify or cancel the Scholarship Challenge. Students will be notified of any changes.
